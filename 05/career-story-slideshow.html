The Trap

Most people think about
income as a straight line.

Year 1 to Year 30, they assume income will simply grow steadily. So they spend steadily. Save a little. Never travel. And arrive at retirement with regret — not wealth.

The Reality

Real income is slow, then fast,
then explosive.

The truth is — income doesn't move in a straight line. It moves in phases. Slow while you learn. Faster once you build. Explosive when you compound.
