Congratulations to Swarna Chitrakar for winning the National Award!

Swarna Chitrakar is a renowned Patachitra artist from Naya village, Pingla Block, West Medinipur. She has represented her art and community at a national and global scale, having travelled to the US, UK, France, and many other countries!

She has spearheaded the economic and social empowerment of other women artists in her village, and is an inspiration to us all.

Swarna Chitrakar Naya Pingla, Patachitra

The exploration of metal in sculptural form has been one of the preoccupations of artist Janardhanan Rudhramoorthy, over the last decade. Deeply inspired by nature, he has found various means to adapt the hard and heavy medium into visibly fluid and dynamic objects. Coming from a background in painting, Jana was drawn to processes of three-dimensionality that translated and presented his concepts in ways beyond the surface of a canvas.

As he expertly pursues potential shapes and figures, Jana's grammar has grown to accommodate visual and material paradoxes. Solid iron is teased into softness, furthering the experience of malleable matter that can bend and turn with ease.

Dense sheets of metal are forged into multiple slender strips, developing the illusion of transparency and graceful movement. Despite the large scale, many forms are sculpted and displayed to emulate lightness and the lack of gravity, defying the nature of the material.
