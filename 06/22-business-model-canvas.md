## 2. Value Proposition

### Faster Research

Reduce days of manual research into minutes through autonomous AI agents.

### Lower Cost

Deliver institutional-quality valuation capabilities without requiring a large analyst team.

### Explainable AI

Every valuation includes evidence, assumptions, calculations, and source references instead of opaque predictions.

### Continuous Monitoring

Valuations update automatically as earnings reports, filings, macroeconomic events, or news become available.

### Multi-Method Analysis

Generate DCF, Comparable Company Analysis, Precedent Transactions, Sum-of-the-Parts, and scenario-based valuations from the same research pipeline.

### Democratization of Finance

Give retail investors access to research capabilities traditionally available only to large institutions.

# Strategic Differentiator

The core competitive advantage is not merely generating valuations but orchestrating a team of specialized AI agents that independently research, verify, challenge assumptions, and synthesize publicly available information into transparent, continuously updated investment analyses.

In effect, the platform aims to function as an autonomous equity research department available on demand, capable of scaling across thousands of publicly traded companies while maintaining traceable reasoning and evidence-backed conclusions.
