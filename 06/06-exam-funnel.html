06
The Better Strategy

A Portfolio Approach

Attempt the exam — if you genuinely want it, go for it
Simultaneously build marketable skills in parallel
Build proof of work — projects, portfolios, evidence
Explore adjacent careers — stay curious and open
Keep multiple doors open — so if you're in the 99%, you are not starting from zero
07
Reframe

Ask a better question

Most students ask
"What is everyone else attempting?"
Better question
"Given my strengths, where do I have an unusually good chance?"

This is why capability vs compatibility matters. A student may be capable of competing — but may have much better odds and a happier life in a different domain.
